Learning & Care

Schools and hospitals

Public schools Two school districts — Taytay I and Taytay II — run the town's public elementary and high schools, including Taytay National High School and Taytay Senior High School.
Colleges University of Rizal System, Siena College of Taytay, ICCT Colleges, Juan Sumulong Memorial Junior College, Asian Institute of Computer Studies and more.
Hospitals Taytay Emergency Hospital (24/7, at the Municipal Hall), Manila East Medical Center, Taytay Doctors Hospital and RPHS Taytay Annex.

Frequently asked questions

What are the Municipal Hall's office hours?

Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, except public holidays.

Can I pay my real property tax online?

Yes. Use the Real Property Tax service on e-BOSS at online.taytayrizal.gov.ph.

Who do I call in an emergency?

Call Sagip Taytay at 0985 488 3352, or the MDRRMO at 0952 619 9511 / 0970 884 3791.

Where can I find job openings?

PESO Taytay posts openings and job fairs on its Facebook page.

Have a concern or feedback?

Tell the municipality — we will point you to the right office.
