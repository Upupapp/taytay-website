Who does what

Offices & Departments

  • Office of the MayorExecutive leadership, programs and the municipal administration.
  • Sangguniang BayanThe town council — ordinances, resolutions and sessions.
  • Business Permits & LicensingNew and renewed business permits, also online through e-BOSS.
  • Municipal Treasurer's OfficeReal property tax and other local tax payments.
  • Municipal Civil RegistrarBirth, marriage and death records.
  • Municipal Health OfficePublic health programs and the Taytay Emergency Hospital at the Municipal Hall.
  • Social Welfare & DevelopmentAssistance for families, seniors, persons with disability and children.
  • MDRRMODisaster risk reduction and the Sagip Taytay command center.
  • PESO TaytayJob listings, job fairs and employment referrals.
  • Gender & Development OfficeGAD programs and gender sensitivity training.
  • Agriculture Office & FITS CenterFarm support and the Techno Gabay program.
  • Public Information OfficeNews, advisories and inquiries — information@taytayrizal.gov.ph.
How It Works

Requesting a document or permit

  1. 01Check the requirementsSee what the office needs and print any forms before you go.
  2. 02Submit your requestFile at the office in the Municipal Hall, or online for e-BOSS services.
  3. 03Pay the feesSettle any fees at the Treasurer's Office or through the online portal.
  4. 04Claim your documentPick up your document or permit once it is released.
