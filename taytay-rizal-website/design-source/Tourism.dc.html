Tourism & Culture

Garments, crafts and a hilltop parish

Shop where the country's ready-to-wear is made, then visit a parish that has served the town since 1579.

Plan Your Visit
Destinations

Places to explore

No. 01

Taytay Tiangge

The Municipal Tiangge and garment centers like Bagpi and Igpai fill the Club Manila East Compound every Tuesday and Friday.

No. 02

Minor Basilica of St. John the Baptist

Taytay Church — moved uphill from the lakeshore by the Jesuits, first built in stone in 1599–1601, and declared a minor basilica in 2024.

No. 03

Club Manila East

Home to the Funtasy Lagoon, the Oceanwaves wave pool and a 360-meter zipline — and the garment centers in its compound.

No. 04

Taytay Kalayaan Park

The town's open-air plaza and stage, where civic programs and celebrations gather.

No. 05

Furniture & Woodcraft Shops

Doors, furniture and wood products from the makers behind Taytay's woodworks name.

No. 06

Taytay Public Market

The New Taytay Public Market, a daily stop for Taytayeños shopping for food and everyday goods.

Every February

HaMaKa Festival

Hamba, Makina at Kabuhayan — a week of masks, feathers and street dancing for the woodworkers and garment makers who built the town.

Getting Started

Plan your visit

  1. 01ArriveTaytay is about 20 km from Manila, on the Metro Manila side of Rizal.
  2. 02ShopTime your trip for a Tuesday or Friday to catch the tiangge at its busiest.
  3. 03VisitWalk up to Taytay Church, now a minor basilica, and the plaza at Kalayaan Park.
  4. 04CelebrateCome back for HaMaKa in February or the feast of St. John the Baptist.
