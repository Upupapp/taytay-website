Governance

How a barangay is run

  1. 01Punong BarangayThe elected barangay captain, who leads the barangay and its programs.
  2. 02Sangguniang BarangayThe barangay council that passes local ordinances and the budget.
  3. 03Sangguniang KabataanThe youth council that runs programs for young Taytayeños.
  4. 04Tanod & RescueBarangay peacekeepers and rescue teams, the first responders on your street.
