# Data and Sources

This file lists the facts used on the site and where they came from. The research was done in November 2026. The full text of every page is in `docs/page-text/`.

The site's content structure follows the reference municipal site esperanza-website.netlify.app, without its DOST section.

## Main sources

- **Official LGU website:** https://www.taytayrizal.gov.ph/ — officials, offices, hotlines, Municipal Hall address, e-BOSS services, vision and mission, news posts (October–November 2026), barangay directory.
- **e-BOSS portal:** http://online.taytayrizal.gov.ph — new business registration, online renewal, real property tax.
- **Philippine Statistics Authority, 2024 Census of Population (POPCEN)** — total population of 397,111 and the barangay populations, also cross-checked on PhilAtlas: https://www.philatlas.com/luzon/r04a/rizal/taytay.html
- **DTI Cities and Municipalities Competitiveness Index (CMCI):** https://cmci.dti.gov.ph — Taytay ranked 2nd among 1st- and 2nd-class municipalities in 2024.
- **Wikipedia, "Taytay, Rizal" and "Taytay Church":** history, the 1579 founding, the move uphill and the "San Juan del Monte" name, the stone church of 1599–1601, the 2024 minor basilica declaration, the 1901 Province of Rizal (Act No. 137, June 11), and P.D. 824 (1975).
- **LGU Taytay Facebook pages** — PIO, MDRRMO, PESO and GAD (linked from the Contact page).

## ⚠️ Confirm before publishing as an official site

1. **Barangay captains.** These are the current incumbents and may change after the next Barangay and SK Elections (BSKE).
2. **Name spellings.** Dolores captain: "Reinier" vs "Reiner" Pacleb. Also Roseller Z. Valera's middle initial.
3. **Office hours.** The site shows 8 AM – 5 PM. Some official sources list 8 AM – 7 PM.
4. **Town fiesta date (June 24).** This was inferred from the Feast of St. John the Baptist and "Basaan" photo captions. Confirm the exact HaMaKa week each year.

6. **Officials' photos.** Officials are shown with initials. Add official portraits if the PIO provides them.
7. **Image permissions.** See `docs/IMAGES.md`. Confirm you have the right to use each photo, and credit the photographers if required.
