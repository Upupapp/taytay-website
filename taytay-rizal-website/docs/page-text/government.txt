WHO DOES WHAT
Offices & Departments
Office of the Mayor
Executive leadership, programs and the municipal administration.
Sangguniang Bayan
The town council — ordinances, resolutions and sessions.
Business Permits & Licensing
New and renewed business permits, also online through e-BOSS.
Municipal Treasurer's Office
Real property tax and other local tax payments.
Municipal Civil Registrar
Birth, marriage and death records.
Municipal Health Office
Public health programs and the Taytay Emergency Hospital at the Municipal Hall.
Social Welfare & Development
Assistance for families, seniors, persons with disability and children.
MDRRMO
Disaster risk reduction and the Sagip Taytay command center.
PESO Taytay
Job listings, job fairs and employment referrals.
Gender & Development Office
GAD programs and gender sensitivity training.
Agriculture Office & FITS Center
Farm support and the Techno Gabay program.
Public Information Office
News, advisories and inquiries — information@taytayrizal.gov.ph.
HOW IT WORKS
Requesting a document or permit
01
Check the requirements
See what the office needs and print any forms before you go.
02
Submit your request
File at the office in the Municipal Hall, or online for e-BOSS services.
03
Pay the fees
Settle any fees at the Treasurer's Office or through the online portal.
04
Claim your document
Pick up your document or permit once it is released.
