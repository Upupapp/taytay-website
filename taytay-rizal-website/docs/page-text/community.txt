LEARNING & CARE
Schools and hospitals
Public schools
Two school districts — Taytay I and Taytay II — run the town's public elementary and high schools, including Taytay National High School and Taytay Senior High School.
Colleges
University of Rizal System, Siena College of Taytay, ICCT Colleges, Juan Sumulong Memorial Junior College, Asian Institute of Computer Studies and more.
Hospitals
Taytay Emergency Hospital (24/7, at the Municipal Hall), Manila East Medical Center, Taytay Doctors Hospital and RPHS Taytay Annex.
